**全文（中英对照）:**

**Stock Market Today: Dow, S&P Live Updates for November 10**

**今日股市：11 月 10 日道指与标普实时行情更新**

Stocks rose, while bonds and the yen fell, as hopes for a deal to end the longest US government shutdown boosted appetite for risk.

随着结束美国史上最长政府停摆协议的希望提振风险偏好，股市上涨，债券和日元下跌。

Contracts for the S&P 500 and the Nasdaq 100 rose 0.4% and 0.6%, as Senate Republican leader John Thune said a deal is “coming together,” planning a test vote on a narrow spending package to end the 40-day shutdown.

标普500指数期货上涨0.4%，纳斯达克100指数期货上涨0.6%。参议院共和党领袖约翰·图恩表示，结束为期40天停摆的支出协议“正在成形”，并计划进行测试性投票。

Asian shares advanced, led by South Korea. The 10-year US Treasury yield climbed more than two basis points to 4.12%, while the yen fell 0.2% against the dollar.

亚洲股市普遍上涨，其中韩国领涨。10年期美债收益率上升逾2个基点至4.12%，日元兑美元下跌0.2%。

Despite the optimism, markets remained cautious after last week’s tech stock sell-off reignited valuation concerns. Asian tech shares, which have outperformed US peers this year on AI optimism, were particularly vulnerable.

尽管乐观情绪升温，但上周科技股暴跌重新引发对高估值的担忧。此前因人工智能乐观情绪而领跑的亚洲科技股尤为脆弱。

“The week ahead will depend on whether the US government can orchestrate an end to the shutdown,” said Kyle Rodda, a senior analyst at Capital.com. “Friday’s rally was merely putting lipstick on the proverbial pig.”

Capital.com高级分析师凯尔·罗达表示：“未来一周市场走向取决于美国政府能否结束停摆。周五的反弹只不过是粉饰太平。”

The Senate will hold a procedural vote on Sunday. If passed, all senators must consent for a quick reopening. The House would then need to approve the bill, with Speaker Mike Johnson giving lawmakers two days’ notice to return.

参议院将于周日举行程序性投票。若通过，需全体议员同意方能快速重启政府。众议院随后需批准该法案，议长迈克·约翰逊表示将提前两天通知议员返岗。

Chinese assets will be in focus after consumer prices unexpectedly rose 0.2% in October, driven by holiday demand for travel, food, and transport, while factory-gate deflation eased.

中国资产受到关注，因10月消费者价格意外同比上涨0.2%，节日旅游、食品与交通需求推动价格上升，同时工厂出厂价降幅收窄。

The S&P 500 rose 0.1% on Friday, rebounding from its 50-day moving average after US consumer sentiment fell to a three-year low. The Bloomberg Dollar Index edged up 0.1%.

标普500指数周五上涨0.1%，在美国消费者信心降至三年低点后自50日均线反弹。彭博美元指数上涨0.1%。

CBA strategist Joseph Capurso wrote: “Even if the shutdown ends this week, data releases will take time to resume. Several Fed officials have signaled reluctance to cut rates further without key data.”

澳大利亚联邦银行策略师约瑟夫·卡普尔索表示：“即使停摆结束，数据恢复仍需时间。多位美联储官员在关键数据缺失的情况下不愿进一步降息。”

英伟达CEO黄仁勋要求台积电增加芯片供应，以满足强劲的AI需求。
