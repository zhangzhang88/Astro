**Trump Backs Deal to End US Shutdown as Senate Plans Vote on Bill**

**特朗普支持结束美国停摆协议 参议院拟对法案进行投票**

President Donald Trump on Monday expressed support for the bipartisan deal to end the US shutdown, a key development that makes it likely the government reopens within days.

唐纳德·特朗普总统周一表态支持两党达成的结束美国政府停摆协议，这一关键进展意味着联邦政府有望在数日内恢复运转。

“We have support from enough Democrats, and we’re going to be opening up our country,” Trump told reporters. “It’s too bad it was closed, but we’re going to be opening up our country very quickly.”

特朗普对记者表示：“我们已经获得足够多民主党人的支持，国家即将重新开放。停摆很不幸，但我们将非常迅速地重启国家。”

Senate Majority Leader John Thune said he expects the president to sign it into law once Congress passes the legislation.

参议院多数党领袖约翰·图恩表示，一旦国会通过该法案，预计总统将签署使其成为法律。

A final Senate vote to pass the spending bill is expected late Monday. It then goes to the House for approval, likely Wednesday.

预计参议院将于周一晚间对支出法案进行最终表决通过，随后将于周三提交众议院审批。

House Speaker Mike Johnson said he would give House lawmakers 36 hours’ notice to return to the Capitol once the Senate passes the bill.

众议院议长迈克·约翰逊表示，待参议院通过法案后，他将提前36小时通知众议院议员返回国会大厦。

Flight disruptions and food aid delays are likely to persist until the shutdown officially ends. Still, Republicans on Monday took a victory lap, with Johnson telling reporters that the shutdown “nightmare,” now in its 41st day, is finally coming to an end.

航班中断和食品援助延迟的情况很可能持续至政府停摆正式结束。不过，共和党人周一已开始庆祝胜利，约翰逊告诉记者，这场已持续41天的停摆“噩梦”终于要落幕了。

The moderate senators’ deal failed to deliver the extension of the Affordable Care Act subsidies that Democrats staked their shutdown fight on, provoking a furious backlash within the party.

温和派参议员达成的协议未能兑现民主党人所坚持的《平价医疗法案》补贴延期，引发党内强烈反弹。

California Democratic Governor Gavin Newsom called the deal “pathetic,” while Illinois Governor JB Pritzker slammed Republican concessions as an “empty promise.”

加州民主党州长加文·纽森称该协议“可悲”，而伊利诺伊州州长JB·普利兹克则抨击协议中的共和党让步是“空头支票”。

Under the agreement, Congress would pass full-year funding for the Departments of Agriculture, Veterans Affairs, and Congress itself, while funding other agencies through Jan. 30.

根据协议，国会将为农业部、退伍军人事务部和国会本身提供全年资金，同时为其他机构提供资金至1月30日。

The bill would provide pay for furloughed government workers, resume withheld federal payments to states and localities, and recall agency employees who were laid off during the shutdown. It would also ban federal layoffs through Jan. 30.

该法案将为被迫无薪休假的政府雇员提供薪资补偿，恢复被扣留的联邦拨款，并召回在政府停摆期间被解雇的雇员。法案还规定在1月30日前禁止联邦机构实施裁员。

Given that the House has to return to Washington and has promised to give lawmakers advance notice, the government is most likely to reopen toward the end of the week.

考虑到众议院需返华盛顿且已承诺提前通知议员，政府很可能在本周末前重新开放。
