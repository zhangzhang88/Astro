**全文（中英对照）:**

**Gold Steady After US Rate-Cut Hopes Spur Biggest Surge Since May**

**金价持稳，此前降息预期推动5月以来最大涨幅**

Gold held gains after recording its biggest daily jump since May, with an imminent end to the longest government shutdown in US history raising the prospect of further interest-rate cuts.

金价录得自5月以来最大单日涨幅后保持涨势，美国史上最长政府停摆即将结束，进一步降息的前景浮现。

Bullion was steady on Tuesday at around $4,120 an ounce, having surged as much as 2.9% in the previous session. A bipartisan deal to end the shutdown in Washington — supported by President Donald Trump — is expected to pass in the Senate, meaning the government would likely reopen within days.

周二黄金价格企稳于每盎司4120美元左右，此前一个交易日曾飙升高达2.9%。由特朗普总统支持的结束华盛顿停摆的两党协议预计将在参议院通过，这意味着美国政府可能在数日内重新开放。

A restart would release long-delayed economic data, providing a crucial temperature check for the economy, and is expected to show a worsening outlook. This in turn would be a catalyst for more central bank easing — a tailwind for gold, which doesn’t pay interest.

政府重启将公布长期延误的经济数据，为经济提供关键的温度测量，预计这些数据将显示经济前景恶化。这反过来又会成为央行进一步宽松政策的催化剂——对不产生利息的黄金来说是一股东风。

**Gold Holds Gains on Rate-Cut Hopes**

**黄金持稳于降息预期带来的涨势**

Bullion is on track for its best annual performance since 1979.

黄金有望创1979年以来最佳年度表现。

Bullion’s blistering rally, which saw it set successive records, ground to a halt last month. Many traders, however, are taking a long-term bullish view. Elevated central bank buying and increased private investments in gold are among the key drivers of growth that continue to support bullion.

上月黄金的迅猛涨势——期间金价接连刷新历史纪录——戛然而止。但许多交易员仍持长期看涨观点。央行持续购金与私人投资增长，仍是支撑金价的重要动力。

Gold was up 0.3% at $4,127.97 an ounce as of 8:33 a.m. Singapore time. The Bloomberg Dollar Spot Index was steady. Silver, platinum, and palladium all gained.

截至新加坡时间上午8点33分，黄金价格上涨0.3%，报每盎司4,127.97美元。彭博美元即期指数持稳。白银、铂金和钯金价格均有所上涨。
